### recommended add: Data Storage

Top Cut currently stores decklists, match history,
event information, and preferences locally on your device.

Top Cut does not currently provide cloud storage,
user accounts, advertising, or cross-device syncing.

### Recommended Third Party Language:
Third-Party Services

Top Cut currently does not integrate advertising networks,
analytics providers, or third-party user tracking services.

If future versions introduce third-party services, this
policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

### Data Deletion:
Data Deletion

Because Top Cut stores data locally on your device,
you can delete your stored data at any time through
the app's settings or by removing the app from your device.

Top Cut does not currently maintain copies of your data
on remote servers.
